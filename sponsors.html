SPONSORSHIP LEVELS

WHAT YOU
GET.

Each level includes every benefit from the levels below it. All sponsorships last one year and must be renewed to keep their benefits.

Diamond$10,000+
  • Priority access and invitations to major events, exhibitions, and competitions
  • Media mentions in press releases, on our website, and across social media
  • Logo banner on the website homepage
Platinum$5,000+
  • Dedicated section on our website for your company and partnership
  • Invitations to Innovation Days: prototypes, design presentations, and hands-on time during the build
Gold$3,000+
  • Invitations to team events and test days
  • Logo on custom-designed team shirts
Silver$1,000+
  • Logo on our banner at competitions, presentations, and public demonstrations
  • Name on the team equipment trailer
  • Name on our solar cars
  • Priority logo placement on the car, with size and placement options
Bronze$500+
  • Tax deduction
  • Sponsor spotlight on our social media
  • Car updates on social media and the website
  • Logo on our website
  • Logo on our 2-time national champion 4-seater and our new 1-seater Advanced car
  • Logo on team race uniforms
Visionary$400+
  • Partner spotlight on social media, shared with schools, universities, and industry
Innovator$250+
  • Logo on team race uniforms, worn at interviews, outreach events, and the race
Supporter$100+

PARTNERSHIP

WHY
PARTNER?

Awareness

Each solar vehicle shows that people can travel without burning carbon. Your brand moves alongside our cars and sparks conversations in the communities we pass through.

Innovation

Students engineer real systems against real race deadlines. Your support invests in the engineers who will shape tomorrow’s technology.

Outreach

Through elementary schools, STEM nights, and community events, HelioTech has reached over 10,000 students. Your support carries that spark into more classrooms.

Publicity

We showcase partners on Instagram, YouTube, and this website. Our work has been featured by NBC and ABC, and in a recent U.S. Army commercial shoot.
