ABOUT HELIOTECH

A STUDENT
MOVEMENT,
POWERED BY THE SUN.

TCHS HelioTech is Temple City High School's student-led solar car program. We design, build, and race solar-powered vehicles, and we share what we learn with our community to inspire the next generation of engineers.

OUR VALUES WHY WE RACE
01

AWARENESS

Greenhouse gases are on the rise. Our solar-powered vehicles show there's a practical alternative to carbon-based fuels and point toward a cleaner, emission-free future.

02

INNOVATION

We pick up next-generation technical skills through hands-on work, teamwork, and critical thinking. Students engineer real systems against real race deadlines.

03

OUTREACH

At elementary schools, STEM nights, and community events, we've reached more than 10,000 students and shown them how engineering and renewable energy work.

OUR STORY 2023 — 2027
OUR STORY

FROM A FIRST CAR
TO BACK-TO-BACK
TITLES.

2023 FOUNDED

HelioTech is founded at Temple City High School with 29 students and one goal: build a drivable solar car. Our first car, “Bob,” was named in honor of the late Bob Westgate (“Hawaiian Bob”), a beloved Dr. Doug Sears Learning Center principal.

2024 ROOKIE SEASON

In our rookie season, we take 2nd place nationally in the Classic Division at Texas Motor Speedway with 293 laps (439.5 miles).

2025 TWO CARS

We race two cars. “Lightning McQueen” wins the Cruiser Division national championship, and “The Roach” takes 2nd in the Classic Division with 445 laps (667.5 miles).

2026 TWO-TIME CHAMPIONS

Our new four-seat Cruiser wins the Cruiser Division on the 30th-anniversary cross-country route, making us two-time national champions.

2027 NEXT

Next, we move up to the Advanced Division with a carbon fiber monocoque single-seater.

HOW WE'RE ORGANIZED TWO TEAMS
STRUCTURE

TWO TEAMS,
ONE CAR.

HelioTech runs like a small engineering company. Every student belongs to a team with real responsibilities.

TECHNICAL TEAM

THE ENGINEERS WHO BUILD THE CAR.

  • Mechanical: chassis, suspension, steering, brakes, wheels, and welding
  • Composites: molds, carbon fiber layups, cores, and body shell
  • Electrical & Solar: battery, motor, wiring, MPPTs, solar array, and encapsulation
OPERATIONS TEAM

THE CREW THAT KEEPS THE PROGRAM RUNNING.

  • Sponsorship & fundraising: sponsor outreach, grants, and giveback
  • Media & outreach: social media, newsletters, events, and school visits
  • Logistics & finance: registration, travel to Texas, budgeting, and invoices
