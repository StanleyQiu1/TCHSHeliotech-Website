ENERGY ARCHITECTURE POWER PATH
FROM SUNLIGHT TO MOTION

ONE PATH
FOR POWER.

Energy travels one way through the car: collected by the array, conditioned, stored, and delivered to the wheels. Each stage is designed to waste as little as possible.

  1. STAGE 1 Solar array

    Converts sunlight into electrical power.

  2. STAGE 2 MPPT

    Optimizes voltage and current to pull the maximum possible power from solar panels.

  3. STAGE 3 Battery pack

    Stores energy and is monitored for safety.

  4. STAGE 4 Motor controller

    Turns driver input into motor commands.

  5. STAGE 5 Electric motor

    Converts electrical power into motion.
