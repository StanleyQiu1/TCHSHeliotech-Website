02 / THE REASON WE BUILDMISSION

A cleaner future.
Built with our own hands.

A solar car is more than a vehicle. It is a place to turn curiosity into practical experience.

HelioTech brings Temple City High School students together around solar vehicles. Our work connects engineering, fabrication, business, and outreach through the challenge of building a car that runs on sunlight.

Students take on real responsibilities, from designing components and wiring systems to finding partners and preparing for competition.
