SOLAR + ENERGY

POWER FROM
THE SUN.

Our solar array manufactured by XXR Solar Energy provides us over 1700W of power to keep our car driving. This power is then transfered to the MPPT which optimizes the power input for all the 72V systems.

ELECTRICAL

CONTROL IS
DISTRIBUTED.

Custom electronics connect vehicle controls, sensors, driver displays, power systems, and telemetry across the car.

MECHANICAL

CARBON FIBER
BULKHEADS.

Instead of using a tubular aluminum chassis as we did for the rest of our cars, we decided to use carbon fiber bulkheads because it reduces a significant amount of weight.

COMPOSITES

FULL CARBON
FIBER AERO SHELL.

The entire shell of this car is made of carbon fiber and was designed and fabricated solely by students amount the composites subteam.
