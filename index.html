/02 WHY WE BUILD MISSION

Engineering is learned
by making it real.

A solar car is a full system problem: electrical, mechanical, structural, aerodynamic, operational, and human.

HelioTech gives students a place to turn classroom ideas into hardware that must survive testing, integration, and competition.

More about HelioTech ↗
